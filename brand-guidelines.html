01 — Foundation

Brand Principles

Every visual decision in ThoughtMetrics stems from four core principles that reflect our positioning as a trusted, data-forward market research platform.

01
Clarity Over Decoration

Data insights must communicate without interference. Visual elements earn their place only when they aid comprehension or reduce cognitive load.

02
Confident, Not Loud

A dark-first palette with calibrated blue accents signals authority and trustworthiness — the same qualities clients expect from research partners.

03
Accessible by Default

Every colour pairing meets WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum. Accessibility is a baseline requirement, not a post-launch audit item.

04
Systematic Consistency

CSS custom properties and the Material Design 3 token architecture ensure every surface and component stays coherent as the product scales.

02 — Typography

Type System

A single, high-quality typeface applied with rigorous weight and size discipline creates visual hierarchy without fragmentation.

Why Plus Jakarta Sans?

Plus Jakarta Sans is a contemporary geometric sans-serif with humanist proportions. Its wide weight range (200–800) lets a single typeface cover everything from display headlines to 10px eyebrow labels without switching families — reducing HTTP requests, maintaining harmony, and simplifying developer decisions.

Its slightly wider letter-spacing at display sizes pairs naturally with negative letter-spacing in headings, producing clean, modern headlines that align with the research-tech sector aesthetic occupied by tools like Typeform, Dovetail, and Maze.

The variable font axis covers all nine weights in a single file, meaning the performance cost is roughly equal to loading two static weights.

03 — Colour

Colour Palette

Built on Material Design 3 colour science. Two complete tonal palettes — dark (default) and light — share the same token names so components adapt via a single data-theme attribute swap.

Palette Rationale

Dark-first: Our research clients and panellists interact with surveys in diverse environments including evening hours and lower-light settings. A dark default reduces eye strain, improves perceived contrast for data-heavy screens, and positions the brand alongside premium analytics tools (Amplitude, Mixpanel, Grafana).

Blue primary family (#adc7ff / #005bc0): Blue communicates trustworthiness, competence, and analytical precision — consistently the highest-performing colour in B2B research-tech UX studies. The light-blue dark-mode primary (#adc7ff) maintains high contrast against dark surfaces while the deep blue (#005bc0) provides authority on white in light mode.

Cyan secondary (#8fd8ff / #006685): A closely related hue that adds differentiation for interactive states and data series without breaking colour harmony. The hue distance is large enough to distinguish but small enough to feel intentional.

Cool grey tertiary (#b9c7e4 / #39475f): Adds a third dimension for tags, chips, and supporting labels without the warmth that would feel out-of-place in a data context.
